# Hero power association table definition

Type: table
Table: hero_power
Scope: table_definition
Summary: Association records link superhero IDs to superpower IDs; joins connect heroes to their recorded powers.
Keywords: hero power, superhero, superpower, association

## Business Summary
Association records link superhero records to superpower records.
## Business Use Cases
Join hero_id to superhero.id and power_id to superpower.id to find powers recorded for a named superhero or heroes associated with a power.
## Data Granularity
One stored hero_id/power_id association per row. Schema declares no primary key or pair-uniqueness constraint; observed data is not a guarantee of uniqueness.
## Data Scope & Exclusions
Only recorded associations are represented; completeness is Unknown / Not verified. Both foreign-key columns are nullable by schema.
