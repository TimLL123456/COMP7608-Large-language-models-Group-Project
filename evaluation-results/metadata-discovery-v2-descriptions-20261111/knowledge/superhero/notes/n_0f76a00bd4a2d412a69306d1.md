# Superpower table definition

Type: table
Table: superpower
Scope: table_definition
Summary: Catalog of power records with integer primary-key IDs and nullable text names; associations to heroes are stored in hero_power.
Keywords: superpower, power, name, catalog, hero_power
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:superpower DDL; schema:hero_power.power_id foreign key; description:superpower.csv:1-2

## Business Summary
Catalog of superpower records, each with an integer identifier and nullable text power name.
## Business Use Cases
Join superpower.id to hero_power.power_id to associate recorded powers with superhero records.
## Data Granularity
One row per superpower record; id is the primary key. Distinct IDs may share labels; uniqueness of power_name is not declared.
## Data Scope & Exclusions
Represents powers in this dataset. Completeness and semantic equivalence of labels are Unknown / Not verified.
