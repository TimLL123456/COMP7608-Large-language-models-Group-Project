# Major department

Type: column
Table: major
Scope: department
Summary: Department text on a major record, usable to group its majors and associated members; independent department entity semantics are unverified.
Keywords: major, department, college, affiliation
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:major.department TEXT; schema:member.link_to_major references major.major_id; values:major department non-null and college-associated examples

## Business Meaning
Department text recorded for a major; whether it denotes an independently maintained department entity is Unknown / Not verified.
## Business Usage
Group majors by department and associate members via member.link_to_major -> major.major_id. A department can be included within a college through its majors' recorded college values.
## Value Meaning
TEXT field; non-NULL in the inspected major records. Examples include English Department, History Department, and Political Science Department; not verified exhaustive.
