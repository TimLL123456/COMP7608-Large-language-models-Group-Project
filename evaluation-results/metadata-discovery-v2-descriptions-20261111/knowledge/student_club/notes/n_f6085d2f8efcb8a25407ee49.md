# Budget entry allocated amount

Type: column
Table: budget
Scope: amount
Summary: INTEGER amount on a budget row; described as amount budgeted in dollars; entries reference events.
Keywords: budget, amount, allocated, planned, event, dollars
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:budget.amount INTEGER; schema:budget.link_to_event REFERENCES event(event_id); description:Budget.csv:5

## Business Meaning
Planned amount budgeted for the budget entry/category associated with an event.
## Business Usage
Use for planned-budget summaries, joining budget rows to events through link_to_event. Multiple entries may reference an event; an event-level total can be formed by summing its entries, though the official aggregation rule is not further documented.
## Value Meaning
INTEGER, in dollars per the Budget field description.

N/A; stored source field, not documented as calculated.
