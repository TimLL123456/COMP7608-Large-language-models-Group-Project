Type: column

## Business Meaning
Area for which a budget amount is allocated; documentation gives advertisement, food and parking as examples.
## Business Usage
Classifies budget entries, which reference events via link_to_event. Can filter/group entries by category.
## Value Meaning
TEXT category label. Observed categories include Advertisement, Club T-Shirts, Food, Parking and Speaker Gifts. Whether a particular food item such as chips is identified within category labels is Unknown / Not verified.
