## Business Meaning
Dollar amount remaining in a budgeted category for an event.
## Business Usage
Represents headroom or overspend for a budget entry; expenses identify budget entries via expense.link_to_budget.
## Value Meaning
REAL amount in dollars; negative values indicate cost exceeded budget.
