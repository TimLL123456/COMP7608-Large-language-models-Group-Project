# Card colors

Type: column
Table: cards
Scope: colors
Summary: TEXT field recording color(s) of a card record; observed codes include B and W, and B is black per supplied Magic color notation.
Keywords: colors, card, color codes, black, white
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:cards DDL colors TEXT; values:cards.colors observed B and W

## Business Meaning
Color or colors attributed to a card record.
## Business Usage
Can group or filter card records by recorded color; exact encoding matters for interpretation.
## Value Meaning
TEXT field. B and W are observed; B denotes black per supplied Magic color notation. Comprehensive encoding, delimiters for multicolor values, and null interpretation are Unknown / Not verified.
## Calculation Meaning
N/A; source/pass-through field.
