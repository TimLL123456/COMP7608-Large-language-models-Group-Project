# Content warning flag

Type: column
Table: cards
Scope: hasContentWarning
Summary: Integer flag for whether the card record has a Wizards of the Coast content warning; documented 0 means none and 1 means sensitive content warning.
Keywords: hascontentwarning, content warning, sensitive content, flag

Sources: schema:cards DDL hasContentWarning INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0; description:cards.csv:22 hasContentWarning definition and 0/1 meanings

## Business Meaning
Indicates whether the card is marked by Wizards of the Coast for sensitive content.
## Business Usage
Can classify card records by presence or absence of a content warning.
## Value Meaning
Documented integer values: 0 means it doesn't have a content warning; 1 means it has sensitive content. Other values: Unknown / Not verified.
