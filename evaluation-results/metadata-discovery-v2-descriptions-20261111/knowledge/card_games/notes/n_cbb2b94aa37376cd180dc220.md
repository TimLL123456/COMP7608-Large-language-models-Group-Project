# Card frame effects

Type: column
Table: cards
Scope: frameEffects
Summary: TEXT field for frame-effect labels distinct from frameVersion; it may capture treatments such as extended art rather than the frame-style year.
Keywords: frame effects, card frame, treatment, extended art
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:cards DDL frameEffects TEXT; values:cards.frameEffects observed NULL and extendedart for Tarmogoyf rows

## Business Meaning
Field for frame-effect labels associated with a card record; distinct from frameVersion, which labels the frame version.
## Business Usage
Can be used separately from frameVersion when categorizing card treatments; do not substitute frame effects for the frame-style year.
## Value Meaning
TEXT, nullable. Observed Tarmogoyf records include NULL and the label “extendedart”; complete vocabulary and definitions are Unknown / Not verified.
