## Business Meaning
Indicates whether a stored card record is a promotional printing.
## Business Usage
Can filter card records marked promotional. This flag does not establish whether a particular language was an official set-release language.
## Value Meaning
INTEGER NOT NULL, default 0; documented values: 0 means not promotional and 1 means promotional.
