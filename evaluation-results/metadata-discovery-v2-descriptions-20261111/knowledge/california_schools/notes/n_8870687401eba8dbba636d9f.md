## Business Meaning
`GSserved` denotes the lowest and highest grades of student enrollment, reported from the most recent certified CALPADS Fall 1 collection.
## Business Usage
Can filter directory records by enrolled grade range; this differs from grades offered (`GSoffered`). Hyphenated spans allow an upper endpoint to be read as the reported high grade, but numeric-grade conversion, kindergarten treatment, and nonnumeric/edge-case handling are Unknown / Not verified.
## Value Meaning
Nullable TEXT; observed formats include ranges such as `K-6`, `1-8`, `7-9`, and `K-12`; a missing value also occurs. Full coding conventions and interpretation of nonnumeric labels are Unknown / Not verified. In joined observations, GSserved and FRPM High Grade sometimes disagree.
