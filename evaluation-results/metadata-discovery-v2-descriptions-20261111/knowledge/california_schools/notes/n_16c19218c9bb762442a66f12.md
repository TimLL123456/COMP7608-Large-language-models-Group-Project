## Business Summary
California directory records carry school identity, county and physical/mailing contact fields, dates, charter/funding fields, organizational classifications, grade offerings, and other attributes.
## Business Use Cases
Supports school identification, contact/location, date, status, and organizational classification analyses. `CDSCode` is the declared primary key and is the school-side join key for FRPM and SAT tables.
## Data Granularity
One row per CDSCode by declared primary key. Uniqueness beyond that declared key is Unknown / Not verified.
## Data Scope & Exclusions
