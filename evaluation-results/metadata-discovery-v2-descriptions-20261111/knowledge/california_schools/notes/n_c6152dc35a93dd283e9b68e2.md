## Business Meaning
Nullable TEXT category classifying a record's school type in the FRPM report.
## Business Usage
The source category list includes `High Schools (Public)`, `Continuation High Schools`, and `High Schools In 1 School Dist. (Public)` as distinct high-school types, separate from `Junior High Schools (Public)`.
## Value Meaning
Observed labels are literal category names, including the categories above; full classification rules beyond these listed categories are Unknown / Not verified.
