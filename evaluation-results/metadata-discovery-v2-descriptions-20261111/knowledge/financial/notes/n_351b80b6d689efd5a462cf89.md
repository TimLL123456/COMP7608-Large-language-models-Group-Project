# Disposition records

Type: table

Summary: Disposition records link clients and accounts; cards reference dispositions through disp_id.

## Business Summary
Stores disposition records linking client identifiers to account identifiers with a recorded type.
## Business Use Cases
Supports traversing card-to-client associations: card.disp_id references disp.disp_id, and disp.client_id references client.client_id; disp.account_id references account.account_id.
## Data Granularity
One row per disp_id, a declared primary key. Uniqueness of client/account pairs is Unknown / Not verified.
## Data Scope & Exclusions
client_id and account_id are required foreign keys to client.client_id and account.account_id. type is required TEXT; observed categories include OWNER and DISPONENT. Role semantics beyond observed labels and status/currentness are Unknown / Not verified.
