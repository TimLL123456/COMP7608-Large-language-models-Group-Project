# Loan account identifier

Type: column
Table: loan
Scope: account_id
Summary: Non-null INTEGER account reference links a loan to its account record.
Keywords: loan, account, foreign key, account_id
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:loan.account_id INTEGER NOT NULL REFERENCES account.account_id

## Business Meaning
Identifies the account associated with a loan.
## Business Usage
Join loan records to account via account_id to obtain the account's statement frequency and branch district.
## Value Meaning
INTEGER NOT NULL; foreign key to account.account_id.
## Calculation Meaning
N/A; source/pass-through identifier.
