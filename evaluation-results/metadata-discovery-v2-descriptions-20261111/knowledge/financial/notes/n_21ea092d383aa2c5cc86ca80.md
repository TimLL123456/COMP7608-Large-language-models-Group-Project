# Disposition client ID

Type: column
Table: disp
Scope: client_id
Summary: Required client identifier on a disposition; references client.client_id and associates the client with an account.
Keywords: disposition, client id, foreign key, client account association
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:disp.client_id INTEGER NOT NULL REFERENCES client.client_id

## Business Meaning
Identifies the client associated with a disposition record.
## Business Usage
Joins disposition records to clients; together with disp.account_id it links a client to an account.
## Value Meaning
INTEGER NOT NULL; foreign key to client.client_id.
## Calculation Meaning
N/A; source/pass-through identifier.
