INTEGER NOT NULL; field description gives unit as US dollars. No special code interpretation verified.
## Calculation Meaning
N/A; source/pass-through field. Sum of loan.amount yields the total recorded loan amount over included loan rows. For an account-level analysis, an account without a loan row contributes zero if explicitly treated as requested.
