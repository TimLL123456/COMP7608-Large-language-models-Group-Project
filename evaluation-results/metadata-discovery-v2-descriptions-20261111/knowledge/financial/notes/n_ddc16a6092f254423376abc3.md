# Account statement issuance frequency

Type: column
Table: account
Scope: frequency
Summary: Account frequency field identifies statement issuance cadence; documented monthly, weekly, or after-transaction values.
Keywords: account, frequency, statement, issuance, monthly, weekly
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:account.frequency (TEXT, NOT NULL); description:account.csv:3 (frequency values and meanings); values:account.frequency (observed categories)

## Business Meaning
Frequency of account statement issuance, according to the supplied field description.
## Business Usage
Can report or group accounts by their issuance cadence.
## Value Meaning
TEXT NOT NULL. Documented values: POPLATEK MESICNE means monthly issuance; POPLATEK TYDNE means weekly issuance; POPLATEK PO OBRATU means issuance after a transaction. These meanings are from the field description.
## Calculation Meaning
N/A; source/pass-through field.
