# Card disposition identifier

Type: column
Table: card
Scope: disp_id
Summary: Required INTEGER association from card record to disposition; account association is reached through disp.account_id.
Keywords: card, disposition, disp_id, account link, foreign key
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:card.disp_id INTEGER NOT NULL REFERENCES disp.disp_id; schema:disp.disp_id INTEGER PRIMARY KEY; schema:disp.account_id REFERENCES account.account_id

## Business Meaning
Identifies the disposition associated with a credit card record.
## Business Usage
Join card.disp_id to disp.disp_id; the associated account can then be determined from that disposition's account_id.
## Value Meaning
Required INTEGER foreign key referencing disp.disp_id.
## Calculation Meaning
N/A; source/pass-through identifier.
