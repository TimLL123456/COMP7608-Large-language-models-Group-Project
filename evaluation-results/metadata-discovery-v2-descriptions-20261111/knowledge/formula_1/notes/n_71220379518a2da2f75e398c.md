# Lap number

Type: column
Table: lapTimes
Scope: lap
Summary: Lap number associated with a lap timing archive record.
Keywords: lap timing, lap number, race lap
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:lapTimes DDL

## Business Meaning
Lap number for the driver’s timing record within a race.
## Business Usage
Identifies the recorded lap sequence in the race timing data.
## Value Meaning
INTEGER, not null, part of the declared composite primary key with raceId and driverId.
## Calculation Meaning
N/A; source/pass-through field.
