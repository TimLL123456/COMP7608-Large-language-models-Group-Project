# Result points

Type: column
Table: results
Scope: points
Summary: Points recorded for an individual constructor-driver race result.
Keywords: results, points, score, race, constructor
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:results DDL and foreign keys

## Business Meaning
Numeric points associated with a driver’s result for a race and constructor.
## Business Usage
Can be aggregated by constructor across selected race results; join results to races and constructors by their respective IDs.
## Value Meaning
Stored as REAL and schema declares default 0; scoring-system interpretation and any exceptional values are Unknown / Not verified.
## Calculation Meaning
Source/pass-through field; aggregation such as summing points is an analysis operation, not a stored calculation.
