Type: column

## Business Meaning
Points value recorded in a constructor standings record linked to a constructor and race.
## Business Usage
Represents constructor standings points at the associated event; observed values vary across rounds within a calendar year and are cumulative in sampled records.
## Value Meaning
REAL, NOT NULL, with schema default 0; no special code/value interpretation verified.
