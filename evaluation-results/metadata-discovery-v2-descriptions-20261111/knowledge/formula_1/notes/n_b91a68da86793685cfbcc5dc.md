# Lap timing race reference

Type: column
Table: lapTimes
Scope: raceId
Summary: Race identifier on a lap timing record; foreign key to races.raceId.
Keywords: lap timing, race identifier, race
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:lapTimes DDL and foreign keys

## Business Meaning
Identifier of the race associated with the lap timing record.
## Business Usage
Links a timing record to race metadata and to race-level results by raceId.
## Value Meaning
INTEGER, not null, part of the composite primary key, and a foreign key to races.raceId.
## Calculation Meaning
N/A; source/pass-through field.
