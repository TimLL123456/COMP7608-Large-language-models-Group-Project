# Lap-by-lap timing records

Type: table
Table: lapTimes
Scope: table_definition
Summary: Lap timing rows associate race, driver and lap number with position and recorded lap time text/milliseconds.
Keywords: lap timing, race, driver, lap, time, milliseconds
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:lapTimes DDL and foreign keys

## Business Summary
Stores lap timing archive records for a driver and race, including lap number, position, elapsed time text and milliseconds.
## Business Use Cases
Analyze recorded lap durations per driver and event, including comparison of lap timing values.
## Data Granularity
One row per raceId, driverId and lap combination; these columns form the declared composite primary key. Race and driver IDs are foreign keys.
## Data Scope & Exclusions
Coverage/completeness and precise conventions for position and absent timing values are Unknown / Not verified.
