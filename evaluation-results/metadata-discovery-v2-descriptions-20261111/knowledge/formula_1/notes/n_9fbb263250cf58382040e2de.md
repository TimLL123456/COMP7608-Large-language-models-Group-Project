# Lap timing driver reference

Type: column
Table: lapTimes
Scope: driverId
Summary: Driver identifier on a lap timing record; foreign key to drivers.driverId.
Keywords: lap timing, driver identifier, driver
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:lapTimes DDL and foreign keys

## Business Meaning
Identifier of the driver associated with the lap timing record.
## Business Usage
Links a timing record to driver metadata and to race-level results by driverId.
## Value Meaning
INTEGER, not null, part of the composite primary key, and a foreign key to drivers.driverId.
## Calculation Meaning
N/A; source/pass-through field.
