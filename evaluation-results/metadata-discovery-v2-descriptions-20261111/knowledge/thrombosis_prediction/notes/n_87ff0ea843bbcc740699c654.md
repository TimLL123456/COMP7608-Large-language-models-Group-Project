## Business Usage
Can distinguish records marked as hospital admitted (`+`) from those marked outpatient (`-`). Other markers are not resolved here.
## Value Meaning
Nullable TEXT. Supplied evidence states a leading plus sign means hospital admission and a minus sign means outpatient follow-up. Other or blank values occur; their meaning is Unknown / Not verified.
