## Business Summary
Stores dated tactical attribute records for teams, including build-up play, chance creation and defence fields.
## Business Use Cases
Use team_api_id to associate attributes with Team; date supports temporal selection of ratings relative to matches. Contains numeric and class-valued tactical attributes.
## Data Granularity
One row per record with integer primary-key id. Duplicate rows by team_api_id and date are verified, so team/date uniqueness is not guaranteed; no rule for selecting among same-date records is established.
## Data Scope & Exclusions
Schema includes team identifiers, date, and tactical rating/class fields. Detailed time coverage and exclusions are Unknown / Not verified.
