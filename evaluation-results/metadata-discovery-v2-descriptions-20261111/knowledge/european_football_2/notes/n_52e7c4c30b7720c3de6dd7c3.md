# Team table definition

Type: table
Table: Team
Scope: table_definition
Summary: Team lookup with API identifiers and long/short name fields; team_api_id is UNIQUE and is used by team attributes and matches.
Keywords: team, team name, team api id, identifiers
Status: provisional
Sources: schema:Team; schema:Team_Attributes

## Business Summary
Lookup table containing team identifiers and long and short team-name fields.
## Business Use Cases
Links Match home_team_api_id and away_team_api_id and Team_Attributes.team_api_id to team records; team_short_name and team_long_name provide compact and long labels. Country is not directly stored; competition country may be inferred from linked match and league records.
## Data Granularity
One row per team record. team_api_id is declared UNIQUE; id is primary key. Completeness and whether every identifier resolves across related tables are Unknown / Not verified.
## Data Scope & Exclusions
Team records represented in this football database. Additional scope or exclusions are Unknown / Not verified.
