## Business Summary
Stores post records with content, recorded dates, ownership/editor identifiers and relationships to other posts.
## Business Use Cases
Associate posts with users via OwnerUserId; inspect dates and content; relate posts through ParentId.
## Data Granularity
One row per post Id; Id is declared NOT NULL, UNIQUE and PRIMARY KEY.
